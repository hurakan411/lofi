Use case: stylized-concept. One completely new tall portrait 9:19.5 pixel-art world illustration for an iPhone lofi focus app. Full bleed, no UI or text.
THE MAIN DESIGN GOAL IS EXPANDING DEPTH: the farther the viewer looks, the wider the world becomes. Foreground starts intimate and narrow, middle distance opens to a substantial shrine precinct, far distance opens into a vast mountain valley with scattered shrine roofs, forested ridges and distant warm lights. A powerful layered view with discovery at every depth, like an expansive environmental game world. Do not make a small enclosed courtyard, a flat shrine facade, or a portrait of a working girl.
Camera: high oblique three-quarter environmental establishing view from a secluded wooden veranda on a wooded hillside. Foreground objects frame only bottom corners; the center and upper two thirds open outward across multiple overlapping elevations into a large night valley. Avoid a giant enclosing roof. A slightly winding descending stone approach links the near hillside shrine garden with a much broader precinct below; glimpses of steps, low stone walls, lanterns and a red torii guide the eye into the distance. Beyond the nearer precinct there are more quiet rooflines between trees, a broader open valley and receding blue mountain ridges under deep indigo starry sky. Make coherent scale and perspective, the architecture shrinking clearly with distance. One nearer wooden worship hall is off-center in mid-distance, not a huge central symmetrical building that blocks the whole view. Let the world visually continue behind it.
Foreground story anchors: in the bottom-left a small human-scale stone basin with a visible dark water surface, one red maple leaf and delicate ripples; a slender bamboo inlet provides a trickle. Basin occupies roughly 16 percent of image width. In the bottom-right, on a small open veranda unobtrusively tucked into the terrain, a VERY SMALL young adult shrine worker sits at a tiny wooden desk drawing with an ink brush on paper under one amber work lamp. The entire seated person is ONLY 4 to 5 percent of the total image height, about 90 pixels in a 1900 pixel tall image. Her head is a tiny pixel cluster, face not detailed. Person + desk together occupy less than 12 percent of image width. Place her fully inside frame with some margin, she is a tiny incidental detail discovered after the landscape. Practical ivory work sleeves and muted vermilion skirt, tied-back dark hair, rear three-quarter view. A couple of papers suggest maple and water ring drawings, no readable text. Do not scale her up to show her activity; the environmental scale is non-negotiable.
Near layers: natural moss, ferns, irregular stone path, sparse autumn maple accents, dark cedar trees, weathered wood and stone lanterns. Preserve patches of spacious darkness and quiet silhouettes. Middle layers: lantern pools on the path, small bridges or terraces only where geography naturally requires, warm shrine windows. Far layers: sparse glimmers of warm lanterns among shrine roof silhouettes in expansive dark forests, atmospheric mountain valley and ridges. Believable tranquil shrine grounds with a subtly magical sense of a world continuing beyond the screen.
Style: mature finely crafted PIXEL ART game environment with visible crisp square pixel clusters, stepped silhouettes, controlled dithering and a coherent pixel grid. Sharp graphic readability and rich detail at different depths, no smooth anime character illustration, no watercolor, no realistic digital painting. Beautiful cinematic lofi night atmosphere. Midnight navy, blue-gray atmospheric distance, muted moss green, aged wood, restrained vermilion accents, small amber light pools. More cool shadow than warm areas. Calm autumn night, no rain. Character must stay extremely small.
Avoid: large woman, foreground heroine, close-up person, detailed face, giant table, oversized basin, giant diagonal roof, boxed-in courtyard, flat postcard composition, one big shrine filling upper frame, straight central staircase filling image, buildings blocking the distant horizon, bright red foliage everywhere, cyberpunk, neon signs, animals, other people, rain, clouds of particles, huge moon, labels, text, UI, watermark, borders, multi-panel layouts.
