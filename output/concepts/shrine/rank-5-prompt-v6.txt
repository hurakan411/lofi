Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset: one full-bleed tall 9:19.5 portrait pixel-art environment for an iPhone lofi focus app, no UI.
Primary request: A fantastical mysterious Japanese Shinto shrine that rises VERTICALLY in MANY LAYERS within one sacred place. The user specifically wants many shrine buildings and torii gates stacked up the image, an unreal vertical shrine world. This must be the defining composition, not a single normal shrine with a tree behind it.
Composition: A vertical architectural labyrinth of beautiful aged wooden shrine halls, vermilion torii gates, stone terraces, short stairways and narrow covered walkways climbing upward through an immense ancient sacred grove. The nearer courtyard occupies the bottom quarter; above it, at least six staggered heights of shrines and torii continue to the top edge. Some shrines are built on mossy stone ledges, others on enormous ancient tree roots and timber platforms, linked by switchback steps and slender elevated corridors. Preserve a coherent visual flow through successive gateways, but allow impossible fairy-tale geography. Strong overlapping layers and smaller distant structures create the feeling that this place continues endlessly upward. Not a mountain panorama, not a distant valley city, not one massive central staircase. Asymmetrical, staggered levels opening into deeper spaces, with small gaps of dark indigo sky and silver-blue mist.
Architecture hierarchy: several distinct wooden worship halls, differing roof sizes and orientations, authentic Shinto shrine details such as shimenawa and white shide; numerous individual vermilion torii at successive vertical levels, including some short rows disappearing behind architecture, rather than all as a single straight tunnel. Elegant weathered tiled roofs, carved cedar beams, mossy stone lanterns and dim warm windows. Architecture and gates fill most of frame, not foliage. Near the top, faint silhouettes of further torii and shrine roofs emerge through mist. Enormous old cedar trunks and roots support and frame these layers, not dominating as one isolated main tree. The main impression is a secret shrine stacked far beyond what should physically fit in one place.
Foreground bottom-left: a modest stone water basin with a single red maple leaf floating on a clearly visible water surface, delicate ripples, a thin bamboo water inlet. Bottom-right: a VERY SMALL working woman seated at a tiny desk on a side veranda, drawing a maple leaf design on paper with a brush, under one amber lamp. Entire seated woman is only about 4 to 5 percent of image height, head a tiny pixel cluster, no face close-up. Practical ivory sleeves and muted vermilion hakama, tied-back dark hair, rear/side view. Desk plus worker under 13 percent of canvas width, entirely visible with margin. She is incidental, a tiny sign of daily life in an immense strange shrine. Do not enlarge her to make details readable. A few fern and red maple accents around nearest courtyard; no giant roof enclosing the view.
Mood: serene and sacred, quietly fantastical, safe and inviting for late-night work, no horror. Warm amber lights scattered carefully across the vertical architecture, cool blue-white mist between levels, deep midnight navy and muted moss green, restrained vermilion torii. A few tiny ghostlike firefly lights far away, very restrained. Lantern light local and soft, no theatrical spotlights.
Style: exquisite detailed hand-crafted PIXEL ART game background with coherent crisp pixel clusters, stepped edges and controlled dithering. Mature lofi atmospheric environment, strong readable shapes and layered depth, not smooth anime illustration, not watercolor or photoreal painting. The viewer can keep finding additional gateways and sanctuaries farther upward.
Avoid: ordinary single-level shrine, one main shrine plus a big tree, vast valley or cityscape, one long central stairway, huge human, foreground portrait, detailed face, giant basin, huge empty sky, mostly foliage, rain, animals, other people, neon cyberpunk, magic portals, huge moon, text, UI, watermark, borders, collage. One coherent scene, tall immersive composition.
